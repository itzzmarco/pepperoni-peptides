Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026 · Terms version 2026-11-02

The short version

  • Without an account, nothing you enter leaves your device.
  • With an account, your log is stored so it can sync between your devices. Only you can see it, plus a partner if you choose to link one.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We never sell or rent your data, and we don't use it to train AI.
  • You can download everything, and you can delete your account and log yourself, at any time, from the app.

Who we are

Peptide Ledger (the "app") is run by __OPERATOR__ ("we", "us"). Questions or requests about your data: __CONTACT__.

We are not a healthcare provider, and HIPAA does not apply to the app. Your log is still health information, so this policy explains exactly what happens to it. It is written to cover consumer health data laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act.

What we collect

If you don't sign in

Everything you enter is saved in your browser's storage on that device only. We never receive it. Clearing your browser data, or "Erase my log" in the app, deletes it.

If you create an account

  • Account details: your email address. If you sign in with Google, also your name as Google shares it. If you use a password, our sign-in provider stores it scrambled (hashed), and we can't read it. We also keep the date you agreed to these terms.
  • Your log (consumer health data): the peptides and medications you track, doses, schedule, vial details, where you got them and who prescribed them if you enter that, each dose you log (time, amount, injection site, notes), and daily check-ins (weight, how you felt, side effects, emergency symptoms and notes).
  • Technical records: our providers keep standard records such as IP address, device and browser type, and sign-in times, to run the service and keep accounts secure.

We collect your log only because you enter it and ask the app to keep it for you. We don't collect location, contacts, or anything from other apps.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sync your log between your devices, show it to you, show it to a partner you linked, and keep accounts secure. We don't use your data for advertising or profiling, we don't sell or rent it, and we don't use it to train AI models.

Who can see it

  • You. The database lets each signed-in person read and change only their own log.
  • A partner you link. If you share or enter a partner code, that person can see your whole log (doses, weights, check-ins and notes), but can't change it. Either of you can unlink at any time, which ends it for both.
  • Our service providers, who process data only on our behalf:
    • Supabase: the database and sign-in.
    • Vercel: hosts the website.
    • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
    • An email provider: only to send sign-in, confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • When the law requires it. We would disclose data only if legally compelled, such as by a valid court order. We would tell you first unless the law forbids it.

We don't share your data with anyone else, including advertisers, data brokers, insurers or employers.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download: on Trends, export a CSV or download a full backup.
  • Correct: edit or delete any entry in the app.
  • Delete your account: open the account menu and choose "Delete account". This deletes your account, your log, your partner links and your codes from the database right away. Copies in our providers' backups, if any, are overwritten on their normal schedule, within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent: deleting your account withdraws your consent and stops all collection. You can keep using the app without an account.
  • Ask us: email __CONTACT__ to confirm what we hold, or to get a list of the providers above. We answer within 45 days. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. If we turn down the appeal, you can contact your state's Attorney General.

These rights are for everyone, whatever state or country you live in.

How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database rules keep each log private to its owner and a linked partner.
  • Signing out removes your log from that device.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever exposes your data, we will notify you as the law requires.

Age

The app is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a minor has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and ask you to agree again in the app before your data is used in a new way.
